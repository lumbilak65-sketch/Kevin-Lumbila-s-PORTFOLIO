About

I'm an IT support professional with nearly three years of Tier 1 and Tier 2 experience helping users in person and remotely. I troubleshoot hardware, software, accounts, access, connectivity, and cloud applications, and I know when to document clearly and escalate complex issues.

I've supported teams in busy service environments using ticketing tools such as ServiceNow, Zendesk, and Jira. My experience also includes Windows and macOS support, identity and access tools, device management, onboarding and offboarding, asset tracking, and security-conscious support practices.

My background in graphic design has played a major role in how I approach technology, development, and problem-solving. I built my own clothing brand by creating and applying graphic design concepts, which strengthened my creativity, attention to detail, and understanding of visual communication.

I also apply those design principles in IT support by understanding the user’s perspective, identifying their needs, and finding solutions that are not only technically effective but also simple and intuitive to use. This combination of graphic design and IT experience allows me to bridge the gap between technology and user experience, creating solutions that are both functional and user-friendly.

A NAME.
A LEGACY.

“Ketel was my father’s middle name.”

Since his passing, creating this brand has become my way of keeping a part of him alive and carrying his name into the future. Every design, every collection, and every idea represents something bigger than fashion.

The name carries a history, but what I build with it is the future. K3tel Division stands for individuality, creativity, and the courage to see things differently.

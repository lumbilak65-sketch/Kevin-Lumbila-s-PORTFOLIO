Your studio, explained.

  1. Pick your form. Choose a garment and change its width, length, and sleeve, flare, or depth.
  2. Build your palette. Set the body, secondary and trim colors, fabric finish and pattern.
  3. Make your mark. Pick a surface, then add text, PNG/JPG/WebP artwork, or your K3TEL logo. Select a layer and drag it on the square surface or use sliders.
  4. Inspect every angle. Drag the 3D model to orbit. Scroll or use +/− to zoom. Front/back/side buttons give quick views.
  5. Keep your work. Download an editable project, studio image, surface artwork or GLB model. Open a project to continue later.

Front/back map to the main body or legs. Left/right map to sleeves, dress panels, or boot shafts. Printed edges can distort near curved surfaces; inspect the 3D view. Templates have editable proportions; custom topology, fabric draping and production pattern drafting are outside this version.
